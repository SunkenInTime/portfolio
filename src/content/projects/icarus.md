## About

Icarus is a free, open-source strategy board for VALORANT — local-first, fast, and honestly just nice to use every day. It's built for players who want to save, test, and iterate on lineups quickly without being blocked by subscriptions or cloud-only workflows. It started as a personal solution to painful strategy planning limitations and has grown into a project used by **1,000+ players**.

Built with a minimal, fast workflow in mind, Icarus focuses on practical planning: place agents, draw routes and utility, and save your work locally so your prep is always available offline.

## Why Icarus

- **Fast**: native desktop, 60fps canvas — the pen draws the second you do.
- **Free**: MIT-licensed, no accounts, no premium tier.
- **Local-first by default**: your strategies stay on your device.
- **Offline workflow**: plan and review without internet dependency.
- **Open-source project**: built in public and open to contributors.

## Current status

- In active development, distributed for **Windows** via the Microsoft Store
- Core mission remains the same: local-first, privacy-friendly, open source
